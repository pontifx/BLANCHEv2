Current boundaries:
- Dork Suite and NERD mode are intended only for targets the operator is authorized to assess; the UI keeps the target explicit, previews exact queries, applies bounded caps, and records warnings.
- Search-operator behavior is controlled by third-party engines and can change. The catalog separates documented, observed, fragile, deprecated, and unsupported claims and records the review date and sources rather than promising permanence.
- NERD supports discoverable HTML search controls, GET/form replay, and reviewed HTTP(S) URL templates containing exactly one {query} placeholder. Sites with inaccessible shadow DOM, anti-automation challenges, authentication gates, unusual scripted widgets, or server-side defenses may require manual setup or may refuse automation.
- Result parsing is generic evidence collection, not a guarantee that every site's presentation can be normalized or that a no-results outcome proves absence.
- Sensitive fixed parameter names are excluded defensively, but operators must still review each discovered surface before saving or running it.
